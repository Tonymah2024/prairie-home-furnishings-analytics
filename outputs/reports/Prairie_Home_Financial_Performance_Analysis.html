Basis of preparation

Simulated accounts of a fictional retailer, generated by double-entry accounting from synthetic operational data.
PolicyTreatment
RevenueRecognised at hand-off (store pickup or loading to the customer's carrier); orders and deposits before that are contract liabilities.
Customer deposits50% on custom, special and scheduled in-stock orders (30% for trade); balance due before pickup; held gross of GST until applied.
DeliveryThird-party delivery is booked and paid by the customer directly - not revenue. Flat-rate delivery collected for a partner carrier is an agent liability.
Protection plansSold as agent; only the retailer's 40% commission is revenue, the remainder is remitted to the insurer.
Cancellations & returnsCustom orders in production retain a 25% cancellation fee; in-stock returns within 3 days carry a 15% restocking fee.
InventoryPerpetual at purchase cost; stocked goods (1200) and goods allocated to customer orders (1210) shown separately.
TaxGST 5%, quarterly; income tax 11% small-business rate on the first $500K, 23% above, YTD true-up; prior year paid 31 March.
Opening positionExplicit 31-Dec-2023 balances (cash, assets, inventory, deposits, payables, debt, share capital); opening retained earnings is the documented residual.
