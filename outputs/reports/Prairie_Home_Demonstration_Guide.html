Prairie Home Furnishings Analytics - Demonstration guide

Demonstration developed by VisiVault Analytics Ltd. · 20-minute walkthrough for a furniture retailer's management team

Synthetic demonstration data — not actual company financial or operational records. Prairie Home Furnishings is fictional; say so at the start of every demo.

Before the meeting

  • Open the dashboard ([Streamlit Community Cloud URL - added after deployment] or locally: .venv/bin/streamlit run dashboard/app.py).
  • Have the Excel model and the executive report PDF ready to share.
  • Ask which systems they already use (POS/ERP, accounting, e-commerce, CRM) - the pilot reads from them; it replaces nothing.

Walkthrough

PageWhat to show
Executive OverviewStart here: bookings vs recognised revenue, open orders and deposits, headline findings and ranked actions.
Sales Performance / Product ProfitabilityFilter by date, category and channel; show promotion lift and the custom vs ready-made margin trade-off; open the cross-sell table.
Inventory IntelligenceFilter to ABC class A and action 'Reorder now'; then show aged stock and the clearance list.
Custom Order TrackingTick 'Only past promised date' - the tracker lists each late order with deposit, balance, supplier ETA and model delay risk.
Supplier & FulfillmentShow the weakest suppliers' on-time %, the Saturday pickup-slot crunch and why third-party delivery is not revenue.
Customer Intelligence / Design ConsultationsExplain low repeat rates in furniture; show the follow-up effect on conversion (32% vs 53%) and the open-quote pipeline.
Income Statement / Balance Sheet / Cash Flow / RatiosShow revenue recognised at hand-off, deposits as a liability, the cash bridge and the cash conversion cycle after deposits.
Forecasting / Scenario SimulatorMove the sliders (volume, margin, supplier delays, payment terms) and watch revenue, profit, cash and working capital update together.

Questions to ask

  • How are deposits, balances and supplier ETAs tracked today, and who chases late orders?
  • Which reports does management review monthly, and how long do they take to produce?
  • Where is stock aging or clearance decided?
  • How are design consultations followed up and measured?
