90-day pilot implementation plan

Demonstration developed by VisiVault Analytics Ltd. · Retail and financial intelligence pilot · October 2026

Plan for a pilot using the retailer's own data. The demonstration that accompanies it uses a fictional retailer and synthetic data.

Deliverables at day 90

  • Live dashboard on the retailer's data (sales, product profitability, inventory, financial statements, cash).
  • Month-end management pack and 13-week cash forecast template.
  • Reorder-point, safety-stock and clearance lists with a supplier lead-time scorecard.
  • Data dictionary, reconciliation log and runbook.

Risks & mitigations

  • Data export gaps - start from monthly exports; automate later.
  • Policy differences (e.g., deposit or revenue timing) - confirm in week 1 and mirror the books exactly.
  • Staff time - limit to 2-3 hours per week from one contact.

Phase 2 options

Customer conversion (design consultations, follow-up, attribution) and order fulfilment (delay-risk scoring, pickup and carrier analytics).
